# Two Pointers

## Concept

Coordinate two positions in a sequence to reduce repeated work.

## Pattern

Move pointers according to the invariant and the input ordering/constraint.

## Recognition Keywords

Pair, sorted, left, right, reverse, partition, palindrome.

## Story

Two people inspect a row from chosen positions and move based on a shared rule.

## Common Mistakes

- Moving the wrong pointer
- Not proving the loop invariant
- Applying sorted-array logic to unsorted input

## Time Complexity

Often `O(n)` after any required preparation.

## Space Complexity

Often `O(1)` auxiliary space.

## Future Connections

Sliding window, partitioning, linked lists.
